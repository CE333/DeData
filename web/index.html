DeDataPrivate data is computed on, never handed over. Buyers pay for privacy budget. opBNB Testnet (5611)

Sell insight, not data.

Datasets never leave their owner. Buyers pay for a scarce differential-privacy budget, results are settled optimistically on opBNB, and every result carries a hidden fingerprint that proves who leaked it. Models trained on the results get a soulbound provenance receipt.

One request, five on-chain steps

RequestBuyer locks payment and buys eps
CommitProvider posts result hash and a 20% bond
ChallengeBuyer has 24h to dispute
SettleProvider paid, 2.5% fee, bond returned
AttributeLeaked canary slashes the buyer's stake

Priced privacy

Price rises as the privacy budget is spent, so scarce data costs more.

Leak attribution

Per-buyer canaries committed in a Merkle root make a leak provable.

Provenance

A soulbound NFT links a model to the datasets it was trained on.

Decrypt a result

Fetches the encrypted result from the provider node, checks its hash against the on-chain commitment, and decrypts it in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
